### Image Delivery Guidelines
1. **File Format:** Deliver original high-resolution JPEG, TIFF, or WebP files.
2. **Colour Profile:** sRGB for consistent web reproduction across wide-gamut monitors and mobile displays.
3. **Naming Convention:** Match the filenames shown above (e.g. `hero.webp`, `portrait-editorial.webp`) to enable direct drop-in replacement.
